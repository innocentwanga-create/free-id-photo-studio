Photo guidance

  • Face the camera directly with a neutral expression.
  • Keep eyes open and face unobstructed.
  • Use even lighting and avoid strong shadows.
  • Keep the head and shoulders within the frame.
  • Check official rules before submitting a photo.

Privacy by design

Your selected image and edits stay in this browser tab. No account or photo upload is required. AI scripts and model files are downloaded from third-party public CDNs when you use the AI tools.
